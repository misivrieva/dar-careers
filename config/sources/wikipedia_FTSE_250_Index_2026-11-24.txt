The '''Financial Times Stock Exchange 250 Index''', also called the '''FTSE 250 Index''', '''FTSE 250''', or, informally, the "'''Footsie 250'''" {{IPAc-en|ˈ|f|ʊ|t|s|i}}, is a [[stock market index]] that consists of the 101st to the 350th [[market capitalisation|mid-cap]] [[blue chip (stock market)|blue chip]] [[company|companies]] listed on the [[London Stock Exchange]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-05-13 |title=MCX |url=https://research.ftserussell.com/Analytics/FactSheets/Home/DownloadSingleIssue?issueName=MCX&isManual=False |access-date=2024-05-13 |website=FTSE Russell |language=en-GB}}</ref>

==Description==
The index consists of 11 [[Industry Classification Benchmark|ICB]] [[business sector|sectors]], three of which had a [[market capitalisation]] exceeding £25 billion as at 31 March 2026. These are Financials, Industrials and Consumer Discretionary, which together account for approximately 74% of the index's market capitalisation.<ref name="constituents">{{cite web |url=https://research.ftserussell.com/Analytics/FactSheets/Home/DownloadSingleIssue?issueName=MCX&isManual=False |title=FTSE 250 Factsheet |website=LSEG |access-date=31 March 2026}}</ref> On the same date, five companies had a market capitalisation exceeding £3 billion: [[Balfour Beatty]], [[Aberdeen Group]], [[Investec]], [[Taylor Wimpey]] and [[Johnson Matthey]], together accounting for approximately 6% of the index's market capitalisation.<ref name="constituents" />

Each calendar quarter, the FTSE 250's constituents are reviewed and some companies exit or enter the index, resulting in irregular [[Volume (finance)|trading volume]] and price changes as [[market participant]]s rebalance their [[Portfolio (finance)|portfolios]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.morningstar.co.uk/uk/news/226246/ftse-reshuffle-whos-in-whos-out.aspx|title=FTSE Reshuffle: Who's In, Who's Out?|website=Morningstar|date=1 September 2022|access-date=15 May 2024}}</ref>

Related indices are the [[FTSE 100 Index]] (which lists the largest 100 companies), the [[FTSE 350 Index]] (which combines the FTSE 100 and 250), the [[FTSE SmallCap Index]] and the [[FTSE All-Share Index]] (an aggregation of the FTSE 100 Index, the FTSE 250 Index and the FTSE SmallCap Index).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.londonstockexchange.com/indices?tab=ftse-indices|title=All Indices |publisher=London Stock Exchange|access-date=24 January 2025}}</ref>

==Record values==
The index began on 12 October 1992 at 2,403, with a market capitalisation of £98 billion. It closed at 24,250.80 on 1 September 2021, a level it did not surpass until 4 August 2026, when it set a new record high.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/index/UK/FTSE%20UK/MCX/historical-prices |title=MCX {{!}} FTSE 250 Index GBP historical prices |publisher=The Wall Street Journal |access-date=6 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Britain's FTSE 250 index hits record high, topping 2021 peak |url=https://www.marketscreener.com/news/britain-s-ftse-250-index-hits-record-high-topping-2021-peak-ce7f50deda80f525 |work=Reuters |date=4 August 2026 |access-date=6 August 2026}}</ref>
